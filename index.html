The Thesis

A dog apothecary for the household that takes its rituals seriously.

Pupproper began on a bathroom shelf. An unopened bottle of Aesop sat next to a half-empty bottle of dog shampoo with a cartoon bee on it. The two products did, structurally, the same thing — clean a surface, leave a scent, ask for thirty seconds of your attention. One was treated as a ritual. The other was treated as an errand.

We started Pupproper to close that gap. Every product is developed with three people in the room: a perfumer, a board-certified veterinary dermatologist, and a designer. We ship slowly. We do one thing at a time.

Why this exists

Three things you've always thought.

No. 01

"Most 'natural' pet products bury synthetics behind a proprietary fragrance line."

Source: standard pet-grooming category labeling practice

Resurrection answers: fourteen ingredients, fully disclosed in standard INCI nomenclature on the bottle. Every one of them GRAS-affirmed. No hidden synthetics, no proprietary blends, no asterisks.

No. 02

"Bottle looks expensive but the spray is watery and the scent fades in twelve hours."

Source: a recurring complaint across premium pet grooming category reviews

Resurrection answers: a higher-viscosity formulation engineered for thick, curly, and double-coated dogs. The bespoke fragrance was built to leave a quiet trail that lasts the afternoon, not vanish by lunch.

No. 03

"My dog is terrified of the aerosol noise — I had to throw the can out."

Source: aerosol-sensitivity affects an estimated 33% of dogs (13,700-dog veterinary study)

Resurrection answers: a silent fine-mist atomizer — no propellant, no aerosol hiss. Pump, mist, set the bottle back down. The dog doesn't notice.

The Ritual

Thirty seconds, three movements.

A coat refresher is a ritual if you decide it is. We've described ours below in the register of a perfumer's instruction card.

Step 01

Mist along the spine.

Hold the bottle approximately 15 cm from the coat. Three short pulls. Resist the temptation to over-apply.

Step 02

Work outward.

A wide-tooth comb, from the spine to the flanks. Calm strokes. Let the rice starch lift the surface.

Step 03

Pause for thirty seconds.

The mist absorbs. The scent settles. The dog notices the change in attention before the change in coat.

What's Inside

Fourteen ingredients. Each listed. Each chosen.

Rice starch + colloidal oatmeal + kaolin clay — the absorbents. They lift the surface of the coat without water and give the mist its luxurious body.

Aloe vera juice + panthenol + glycerin + Centella asiatica — the conditioners and botanical. Leave the coat soft, never sticky.

Lactobacillus ferment lysate — a plant-fermented probiotic complex that supports the coat's natural barrier between baths.

Bespoke fragrance — sandalwood, neroli, vetiver. Developed with an independent perfumer on a brief that began with Le Labo Santal 33 and ended somewhere quieter.

Geogard ECT preservation — paraben-free, EWG-approved, gentle on the coat for the bottle's full 36-month shelf life.
