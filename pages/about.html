Our Story

It began on a bathroom shelf.

An unopened bottle of Aesop hand wash sat next to a half-empty bottle of dog shampoo with a cartoon bee on it. The two products did, structurally, the same thing — clean a surface, leave a scent, ask for thirty seconds of your attention. One was treated as a ritual. The other was treated as an errand.

The dog in question — a Labradoodle named after a film director — did not register the difference. The owner did.

We started Pupproper because the gap between what was on offer for the pet care aisle and what was on offer for everything else on the bathroom shelf had become absurd. Dogs are members of households that spend two hundred dollars on a candle. They are not members of households that should be sprayed with anything that costs less than the candle.

Every Pupproper product is developed with three people in the room: a perfumer, a board-certified veterinary dermatologist, and a designer. The perfumer tells us what the bottle should smell like. The dermatologist tells us what the bottle should not contain. The designer tells us what the bottle should look like on a shelf you don't hide under the sink.

The result is a small line of dog apothecary essentials at an altitude we think the customer has been quietly waiting for. We ship slowly. We do one thing at a time. The first thing is a dry shampoo, called Resurrection. The second is a stain remedy, called Confession. Both are made for the same person.

— Spencer Lampkin · Founder · Brooklyn

The Mark

One bottle. One family. One mark.

The Pupproper mark is built from three locked components: the wordmark, the tagline, and a sitting Labradoodle drawn as flat designed-vector. The same character sits on every bottle we make. Across coral and sage and the four colors still to come, the shelf reads as a family.
