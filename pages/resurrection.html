Resurrection Dry Wash

A waterless coat refresher developed with a perfumer and a board-certified veterinary dermatologist.

$36 6 fl oz · 177 mL

The ritual

A thirty-second ritual for the space between baths. Apply along the spine, between the second and third bath of the week, and work outward with a wide-tooth comb. The mist absorbs into the coat, lifts the surface, and leaves a scent the household notices when the dog walks past on the couch.

What's inside

  • Rice starch & colloidal oatmeal — cleanses without water; supports the coat barrier.
  • Aloe vera juice & panthenol — leaves the coat soft, never sticky.
  • Probiotic ferment lysate — supports the coat's natural microbiome.
  • Bespoke fragrance — sandalwood, neroli, vetiver. Built with an independent perfumer on a brief that began with Le Labo Santal 33.

Full ingredient list

Aqua, Oryza sativa (rice) starch, Glycerin, Avena sativa (oat) kernel flour, Polysorbate 20, Aloe barbadensis leaf juice, Parfum, Caprylhydroxamic acid, Glyceryl caprylate, Panthenol, Citric acid.

How to use

  1. Hold bottle approximately 15 cm from coat.
  2. Mist along the spine. Work outward with a wide-tooth comb.
  3. Allow thirty seconds to absorb.
  4. Resume the afternoon.

Pet safety

Free of phenols, tea tree, eucalyptus, pennyroyal, wintergreen, citronella, and the other essential oils dogs should not encounter. IFRA pet-topical compliant. For external use on dogs only. Avoid eyes.

Why it exists

Dry shampoo that doesn't smell like a science experiment.

Pet dry shampoo has been culturally undervalued for fifty years. Resurrection is what would have existed if anyone had bothered to put a perfumer, a veterinary dermatologist, and a designer in the same room.
