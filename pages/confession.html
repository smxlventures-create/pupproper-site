Confession Stain Remedy

A plant-derived enzymatic spray for the contents of an unfortunate afternoon.

$28 16 fl oz · 473 mL

The ritual

Confession resolves the trace. The formula is enzymatic — a blend of plant-derived proteases that break down organic accidents at the molecular level rather than masking them. Urine. Vomit. The after-effects of an unfamiliar treat. The fragrance leans cleaner — bergamot at the top, cedar at the base, cypress through the middle.

Why it solves the humidity problem

The single most common complaint about stain removers is the "smell comes back when wet" phenomenon. It happens because most formulations only neutralize odor compounds on the surface — uric acid crystals stay embedded and reactivate the moment humidity rises. Confession uses a higher enzyme load (subtilisin protease + lipase) that breaks the crystal structure itself. The trace is gone, not masked. It does not come back at the next rainstorm.

What's inside

  • Plant-derived enzymatic complex — subtilisin protease, alpha-amylase, lipase. Breaks proteins, starches, fats. Active until dry.
  • Lauryl + decyl glucoside — gentle cleansing surfactants from coconut.
  • Stabilizer system — calcium chloride + glycerin holds enzyme activity through the bottle's 18-month shelf life.
  • Bespoke fragrance — cypress, bergamot, cedar. Reads cleaner than Resurrection; built as a complement.

Full ingredient list

Aqua, Plant-derived enzymatic complex (Subtilisin protease, Alpha-amylase, Lipase), Lauryl glucoside, Decyl glucoside, Glycerin, Sodium formate, Calcium chloride, Parfum, Sodium benzoate, Citric acid.

How to use

  1. Blot accident with cloth until dry to the touch.
  2. Saturate area generously with Confession.
  3. Allow eight minutes for the enzymes to engage.
  4. Blot again. Air dry.

Safe across fabric, carpet, and most finished hardwood. Test in an inconspicuous corner first.

Pet safety

Plant-derived enzymes only. Free of bleach, ammonia, phenols, and the essential oils dogs should not encounter. Pet-safe once dry. For surface use only.

Why it exists

A bottle worth leaving on the side table.

Pet stain removers have been industrial, ugly, and slightly toxic-smelling for as long as anyone can remember. Confession is the version you'd actually keep visible — beside the rug, beside the candle, in plain view.

Why it's different

For the rug. For the room. For the dog.
